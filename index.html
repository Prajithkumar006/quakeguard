Earthquake Safety Guide

Life-saving protocols, step-by-step checklists, and offline emergency survival kits.

BEFORE (Preparation)

  • Secure heavy furniture and appliances to the wall so they can't fall.
  • Establish an emergency family meeting spot and communication plan.
  • Maintain a 72-hour emergency kit with water, food, flashlight, and first aid.

DURING (DROP, COVER, HOLD ON)

  • DROP down onto your hands and knees immediately.
  • COVER your head and neck under a sturdy table or desk.
  • HOLD ON to your shelter until all ground shaking ceases.

AFTER (Evacuation)

  • Check your home for gas leaks, broken pipes, and electrical hazards.
  • Evacuate calmly to designated open-air emergency relief shelters.
